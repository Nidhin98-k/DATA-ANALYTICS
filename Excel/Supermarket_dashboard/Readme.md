# Super Market Sales Performance Dashboard

An interactive **Microsoft Excel Sales Performance Dashboard** designed to analyze supermarket sales across products, cities, branches, customer types, and payment methods.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze supermarket sales data and present key business performance metrics through an interactive Excel dashboard.

The dashboard helps identify sales trends, top-performing product lines, customer behavior, branch performance, and preferred payment methods.

## 📌 Key Features

- **Total Sales**
- **Gross Income**
- **Quantity Sold**
- **Average Customer Rating**
- **Top-Selling Product Line**
- **Monthly Sales Trend**
- **Sales by Product Line**
- **Gross Income by Product Line**
- **Sales by City**
- **Sales by Customer Type**
- **Sales by Payment Method**
- **Sales by Branch**
- **Interactive Month Filter**
- **Product Line Filter**
- **City Filter**
- **Payment Method Filter**

## 📈 Analysis Areas

The dashboard provides insights into:

- Monthly sales performance
- Product-line sales and gross income
- City-wise sales performance
- Customer-type distribution
- Payment method usage
- Branch-wise sales
- Top-performing product categories
- Customer ratings
- Overall sales and quantity performance
